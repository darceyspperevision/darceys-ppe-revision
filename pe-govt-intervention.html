Principles of Government Intervention and Limitations

Week 4 • Michaelmas 2026 • Tutor: Dr Richard Povey

Mini-Essays — 750–1000 words for each question

✦ 1. To what extent do results from the theory of the second best depend on the assumption of interdependence of markets? ✦
✦ 2. Countries typically employ many different taxes to raise tax revenue. Is it possible that the optimal mix of the personal income tax, consumption taxes and the corporate income tax could differ between two countries where one has full compliance on all taxes (for some reason), while the other has large problems with non-compliance? Discuss. ✦

Past Exam Questions

  1. Give a statement of the Theory of the Second Best. Illustrate with one example and briefly discuss the relevance of the theory for policy making. (2025, Part A)
  2. Do you agree with a proposal to remove income tax and replace it with tariffs? Why or why not? (2025)
  3. Explain the implications of the Diamond and Mirrlees production efficiency result for the desirability of import tariffs. Are these implications relevant in practice? Why or why not? (2024, Part A)
  4. To what extent do results from the theory of the second best depend on the assumption of interdependence of markets?
  5. Consider a small open economy. The economy exports a good x₁. The good is produced by price-taking firms and consumed abroad and at home. Production of the good generates pollution e. Each unit of output generates one unit of pollution, i.e., e=x. The government wants to reduce pollution and is considering two alternatives: a production tax or an export tax. Which of the two would you recommend and why?
  6. Discuss the challenges introduced by non-compliance when evaluating the desirability of a particular tax policy.
  7. Countries typically employ many different taxes to raise tax revenue. Is it possible that the optimal mix of the personal income tax, consumption taxes and the corporate income tax could differ between two countries where one has full compliance on all taxes (for some reason), while the other has large problems with non-compliance? Discuss.
