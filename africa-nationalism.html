Nationalism and Decolonisation

Topic 1: Nationalism and Decolonisation

African nationalist movements emerged in the mid-twentieth century as a response to European colonial rule, drawing on a range of ideological traditions — pan-Africanism, socialism, liberalism — and mass mobilisation strategies. The urgency of achieving independence often required forging broad coalitions that suppressed internal divisions. A central question is how the political logic of anti-colonial struggle shaped the institutional forms that emerged after independence, and in particular whether the imperative of national unity contributed to the concentration of power in single-party states.

Essay Question

  1. To what extent did the urgent need for ‘national unity’ during the anti-colonial struggle result in the subsequent rise of one-party states?
