Public Economics — Tutor: Dr Richard Povey

📄 Tutorial Readings & Past Questions 2026–27

📄 Michaelmas 2026 Work Scheme (tutorial questions)

Tutorials are with Dr Richard Povey, Michaelmas 2026. Each week is one essay of 1500–2000 words (Weeks 4 and 8 are two mini-essays of 750–1000 words each), using models, diagrams and mathematics, with citations for any empirical evidence. Hand in as a PDF by email by 9am on the day of the tutorial. Particularly recommended readings are starred (*) on each topic page.

Textbooks

  • Connolly & Munro — Economics of the Public SectorUsed in Weeks 1, 3, 5, 6, 7
  • Hindriks & Myles — Intermediate Public EconomicsUsed in Weeks 1, 2, 3, 4, 6
  • Myles — Public EconomicsUsed in Weeks 2, 3, 6
  • Atkinson & Stiglitz — Lectures on Public EconomicsUsed in Weeks 2, 3, 4
  • Barr — The Economics of the Welfare StateUsed in Weeks 5, 6, 7, 8
  • Miles, Myles & Preston — The Economics of Public SpendingDutta, Sefton & Weale (1999) is reprinted here (Week 7)

Tutorial Schedule — Michaelmas 2026

WeekTopicEssay questionStart with
1Cost-Benefit Analysis“Cost-benefit analysis is a good idea in principle, but it is almost impossible to apply in practice to any important social decision.” Discuss, with examples.Connolly & Munro; Hindriks & Myles; Diamond & Hausman (1994); Brekke (1997)
2Commodity Taxation“Commodity taxes should be inversely proportional to demand elasticities.” “Commodity taxes should be higher for goods that are complementary with leisure.” “Commodity taxes should be lower for goods consumed by the poor.” Are these statements true? If so, how can they be reconciled?Heady (1993); Stern (1987); Atkinson & Stiglitz; Hindriks & Myles; Myles
3Income Taxation & Labour Supply“Theory suggests that the marginal rate of income tax should be low both at the bottom and at the top of the income distribution, and higher in between. In practice, the marginal rate of the combined tax/benefit system displays the reverse pattern.” Discuss.Atkinson & Stiglitz; Hindriks & Myles; Myles; Connolly & Munro; Heady (1993); Brewer, Saez & Shephard (2010)
4Govt Intervention & LimitationsTwo mini-essays (750–1000 words each): (1) the theory of the second best and interdependence of markets; (2) optimal tax mix with full compliance vs. widespread non-compliance.Gravelle & Rees; Slemrod & Yitzhaki, “Tax Avoidance, Evasion and Administration”
5Health CareWould it be desirable to increase competition between healthcare providers? Should a distinction be made between primary and secondary care?Barr; Connolly & Munro; Blomqvist (1991); Le Grand (2002); Propper et al. (2008); Gaynor et al. (2016)
6PensionsWhat are the advantages, if any, of pay-as-you-go (PAYG) pensions over fully funded ones?Barr; Connolly & Munro; Hindriks & Myles; Myles; Barr & Diamond (2006)
7Education“The state should fund and organize high school education but not higher education.” Discuss using theoretical and empirical arguments.Barr; Connolly & Munro; Hoxby (2000); Gibbons, Machin & Silva (2006); Machin & Stevens (2004); Barr (2004); Tiebout (1956)
8Welfare Reform & InequalityTwo mini-essays (750–1000 words each): (1) a tax credit to encourage full-time work (30+ hours); (2) can a universal basic income avoid poverty traps and still alleviate poverty?Barr; Hoynes & Rothstein (2019) on UBI; Blundell (2016) on welfare to work
