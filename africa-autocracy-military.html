Forms of Autocracy II: Military Rule

Topics 4 & 5: Military Rule

Military coups have been a recurring feature of African politics since independence, with the continent experiencing more coups than any other region in the world. Explanations for military intervention range from structural accounts emphasising weak civilian institutions, economic crisis, and ethnic fragmentation, to organisational accounts focusing on the internal characteristics of African militaries. A second and contested question is whether military governments perform better or worse than their civilian counterparts on dimensions such as economic management, state capacity, and respect for rights — and what this implies for the normative evaluation of military rule.

Essay Questions

  1. Why have militaries often seized power in Africa?
  2. ‘Military rule is not more competent or democratic than civilian rule.’ Discuss.
