Gender

Topic 6: Gender

Gender analysis draws attention to the ways in which political institutions, processes, and outcomes are shaped by and reproduce social relations between men and women. In the African context this includes the study of women’s political representation and participation, the gendered dimensions of conflict and peacebuilding, the relationship between customary law and women’s rights, and the role of international norms and donor conditionality in shaping gender policy. A central methodological question is what distinctively feminist or gender-sensitive approaches add to the study of African politics beyond what conventional frameworks already capture.

Essay Question

  1. How does gender analysis enhance our understanding of politics?
